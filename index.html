You can set up Smarters Pro on Apple TV 4K in under 10 minutes. This guide shows every step using a premium IPTV subscription from SubTV IP.

No free trial is available — only instant activation after purchase.

What you need

  • Apple TV 4K (any generation) connected to the internet
  • Smarters Pro app installed from the App Store (free)
  • A paid premium subscription from SubTV IP (subtvip.store) — 1, 3, or 12 months with instant activation
  • Your login details (username, password, and server URL) sent by SubTV IP after purchase
  • Optional: Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) if your ISP throttles streaming — set buffer/cache to 5,000 ms

Step-by-step setup (10 minutes)

  1. Open the App Store on your Apple TV 4K. Search for “IPTV Smarters Pro” and install the app.
  2. Launch Smarters Pro. On the welcome screen, select “Login with your subscription”.
  3. Choose “Login with Xtream Codes API” (the method used by SubTV IP).
  4. Enter the Server URL, Username, and Password exactly as provided in your SubTV IP welcome email. Then press “Sign In”.
  5. After login, the app loads your channel list, movies, and series. Select any category to start watching.
  6. To prevent buffering, go to “Settings > Network > DNS” on your Apple TV and set DNS to 1.1.1.1. Then in Smarters Pro, go to “Settings > Player Settings > Buffer Size” and choose 5,000 ms.
  7. Test a 4K channel or movie. If the stream is smooth, your setup is complete. If not, check your internet speed (minimum 25 Mbps for 4K is recommended by AppleInsider).

If a step fails

  • Login error: Double‑check the server URL, username, and password. SubTV IP support is available via WhatsApp.
  • App crashes: Restart Apple TV (hold Menu + Home buttons until light blinks). Reopen Smarters Pro.
  • Buffering or freezing: Ensure you followed step 6. Also check that your internet plan is not throttled — using Cloudflare DNS helps. A 2023 report by eMarketer notes that ISP throttling is common during streaming peak hours.
  • No 4K streams visible: SubTV IP offers 4K/60 fps HEVC streams. Make sure your TV and Apple TV are set to 4K HDR output. Go to “Settings > Video and Audio > Format” on Apple TV and choose a 4K HDR resolution.
  • Still not working? Contact SubTV IP via WhatsApp for instant help. Remember, there is no free trial — activation is immediate after payment.
